# IA-Tutor Industrial Arts AI Research Hub

## Purpose
A Years 9–13 Industrial Arts research workflow that directs students from the AI Tutor into a structured research page and keeps official Fiji Ministry of Education material distinguishable from AI-generated explanations.

## Supported areas
- Year 9–10: Basic Technology
- Year 9–10: Basic Graphics Technology
- Year 11–13: Technical Drawing
- Year 11–13: Applied Technology
- Materials and workshop practice
- Tools and equipment
- Manufacturing processes
- CAD/AutoCAD
- Safety and OHS
- Design and project work
- Technology, AI and sustainability

## Research workflow
1. Student selects Year (9–13).
2. Student selects Industrial Arts subject.
3. Student selects a topic/material/process.
4. Student enters a research question or asks the AI Tutor to create one.
5. Research view presents source evidence separately from AI explanation.
6. AI generates a structured research draft with source links and references.
7. Student can save the work to the e-Portfolio.
8. If the AI cannot confidently answer, the Human Support action directs the student to message Rohil Dass with the original question and research context.

## Research output
- Research title
- Research question
- Objectives
- Key concepts and terminology
- Fiji curriculum/source context
- Material/tool/process information
- Industrial applications
- Safety/OHS considerations
- Fiji-specific examples where supported by sources
- Technical Drawing/CAD connection
- Practice questions
- Examination-style practice (original, not copied)
- AI-generated summary
- Source evidence
- References
- Downloadable report

## Source policy
Official Fiji Ministry of Education resources should be identified as official sources. Publicly available source material may be linked and summarised subject to its terms. Copyrighted textbooks and examination papers must not be copied wholesale into the application unless permission/licensing permits it. AI-generated explanations must not be presented as official Ministry text.

## Human escalation
When the AI cannot confidently resolve a research question, show:

**URGENT — AI could not confidently resolve this question.**

**Message Rohil Dass**

The escalation payload should include the student's question, selected year, subject, topic, relevant AI context, and timestamp, and should be delivered only through a configured support endpoint.
